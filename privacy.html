Si Vision Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 Nov 2026.

Si Vision Publisher is an internal desktop tool operated by SI VISION STUDIOS (TikTok @si.vision.studios). It is used only by our own team to upload and publish our own original short-drama videos to our own TikTok account.

Data we access: after the account owner signs in with TikTok, the tool uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish only to identify our own account and to upload/publish our videos. We do not access any other user's data.

Data we store: the access token is stored locally on our own computer only. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data to third parties, and the tool contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

Data deletion: the account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions; the locally stored token is then deleted.

Contact: TikTok @si.vision.studios.

隐私政策（中文）：本工具是 SI VISION STUDIOS 内部自用的桌面发片工具，只用于把我们自己制作的短剧影片上传、发布到我们自己的 TikTok 账号。只使用 user.info.basic、video.upload、video.publish 权限；授权令牌只保存在我们自己的电脑；不收集、不出售、不分享任何个人资料。可随时在 TikTok「设置 › 安全 › 管理应用权限」撤销授权。
